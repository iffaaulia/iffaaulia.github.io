A little about me.

I'm a data analyst with a background in medicine. I hold a bachelor's degree in Medicine and a master's degree in Biomedical Science, where critical thinking, evidence-based reasoning, and problem-solving were part of my everyday work.

Over time, I discovered that I was equally drawn to data: understanding numbers, finding patterns, and turning complex information into something clear and useful.

To develop my technical skills, I joined Generation UK & Ireland's 12-week intensive Data Analytics programme. I learned Excel, SQL, databases, analytical thinking, statistics, Python, data visualisation, Power BI, and project management.

I'm now building on these skills through hands-on projects and am open to data analyst opportunities in healthcare and other industries.
